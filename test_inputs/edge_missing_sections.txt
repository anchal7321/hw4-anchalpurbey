Title: Cloud Storage Market Entry Options for Mid-Size Enterprises

To: Operations Leadership Team
From: Anchal Purbey, MBA Candidate
Subject: Preliminary Notes on Cloud Storage Vendor Selection

Analysis:
Mid-size enterprises evaluating cloud storage vendors in 2026 face a market with several mature options and a wide range of pricing structures. The three most commonly considered platforms for this segment are Microsoft OneDrive for Business, Google Workspace Drive, and Dropbox Business.

Key considerations in vendor selection include:

- Integration with existing productivity tools is the most frequently cited decision factor. Organizations already using Microsoft 365 tend to select OneDrive due to native integration and consolidated licensing.
- Storage cost per user varies significantly. Bundled plans offered by Google and Microsoft often undercut standalone vendors on a per-gigabyte basis.
- Security and compliance certifications matter for regulated industries. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 compliance are baseline expectations for enterprise buyers.
- Migration complexity is often underestimated. Moving existing file structures, shared drives, and permissions from legacy systems can take several months and may require third-party tooling.

This analysis is based on publicly available vendor documentation and general industry research. It does not include a formal RFP process or vendor-specific pricing quotes.
